        HOW TO PLAY

        It's June 2004. You and your friends have signed on as volunteer fire lookouts at Cinder Peak: a glass room on top of a steel tower, in the middle of a hundred square miles of forest, for two weeks. The lookout before you quit three weeks into the season. Nobody says why.

        Do your jobs. The list in the top left tells you what needs doing: carry the supplies up, start the generator, check in on the radio, watch for smoke with the binoculars and take a bearing on it with the fire finder. Anyone can do any of it, so split up the work.

        People will come by. What you say to them matters, and so does what you do: lock the trapdoor, bolt the door, don't go down alone after dark. Dispatch will give you a code word. Remember it.
