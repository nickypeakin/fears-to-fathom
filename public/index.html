      HOW TO PLAY

      You and your friends just got home to your apartment on the 50th floor. A man in a long coat rode up in the elevator with you, and followed you down the hall. You locked the door. He knocked.

      Survive until 6 AM. He'll keep coming back to knock. If nobody answers he gets louder, then he starts working on the lock, and if the deadbolt gives, he's in. Opening the door makes him stop: sometimes he's just gone, sometimes he wants to talk, and sometimes he goes and finds someone else to wear.

      Other people knock too: the neighbour from 50D, the building super, a delivery driver, the police. Some of them are exactly who they say they are. Some of them are him. Look through the peephole before you open up. People blink and fidget and look around. Something wearing a person doesn't. Listen to how they knock, and to what they say through the door.

      The chain lets you open the door a crack to see who's really there. Hold it open too long on the wrong person and the chain won't hold. Only open the door all the way for people you're sure about. Turn people away and he may find them later.

      If he gets inside the lights go out. Hide in a closet, a wardrobe, the shower or under a bed, and hold your breath when he's close. He'll take one of you and leave.
